The cluster-robust region treats each drifter as a cluster; the Newey--West region allows serial correlation within each drifter's record. The certified subspace dimension is that of the certified plug-in subspace at $\delta = 0.05$. In the degree-1 class every fitted field $Ax + b$ commutes with the dilation about its fixed point and with itself, so two plug-in defects are zero whatever the data. These generators come first in the nested plug-in subspaces, and in the degree-1 rows the column counts how many of them the region is narrow enough to certify (0, 1 or 2 here). The column is therefore not evidence about the rotation, which is judged by $U$ and $L$, and where it is positive while $U > \delta$ the certified subspace does not contain the rotation. For single summers the cluster-robust region is unidentified in 2016 and 2017, which have fewer drifters (3 and 4) than coefficients (6); for 2018 (10 drifters) and 2019 (15 drifters) it gives $U = 0.117$ and $0.109$ ($L$ and the certified subspace dimension are not reported for these two rows); the Newey--West rows cover all four summers. The last rows bound the rotation defect simultaneously over the classes of degree 1 to 3, assuming the largest contains the truth.
